ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Everyday problems deserve real attention.

Anonymous Campus is a proposed privacy-first platform where students can share feedback about hostel and campus facilities without unnecessarily exposing their identity. It helps identify recurring problems through aggregated feedback and allows facility teams to communicate the actions they take.

WHAT NEEDS TO IMPROVE?

Focus on the facilities students use every day.

From basic hostel necessities to sports facilities, students should have a safer way to report problems and share their experiences.

PRIVACY COMES FIRST

Your feedback should focus on the problem.

Ordinary feedback does not require your name, roll number, phone number or email address. The platform is designed to show aggregated concerns rather than publicly expose individual student submissions.

Explore Our Privacy Principles

This is a prototype concept, not a guarantee of absolute anonymity. Technical and operational privacy risks must be tested before deployment.
