ABOUT ANONYMOUS CAMPUS

A Better Campus Starts With Your Voice.

Anonymous Campus is a student feedback platform designed to help improve campus facilities through honest feedback. It gives students a structured way to report problems without publicly revealing their identity.

Every Student Deserves to Be Heard.

OUR MISSION

Why We Built This Platform

Students experience campus facilities every day. However, reporting problems and getting them addressed can sometimes be difficult. Anonymous Campus aims to make this process more accessible by providing a dedicated platform where students can share feedback, report recurring issues and follow their progress.

HOW IT WORKS

From Feedback to Improvement

The platform provides a structured process for collecting, reviewing and addressing student feedback.

01

Create an Account

Register using your eligible college email account.

02

Share Feedback

Select a facility, provide a rating and describe your experience.

03

Review and Monitoring

Feedback and recurring facility concerns can be reviewed by authorised administrators.

04

Track Progress

Students can follow the status of reported facility issues.

PRIVACY AND SECURITY

Your Voice Matters. Your Privacy Matters Too.

Feedback is designed to be anonymous to other students. The platform separates feedback from publicly visible student identity and uses backend-controlled access for authorised administrative processes.

Anonymous feedback does not mean that account-related information is never processed. Access to protected information is restricted through the platform's backend.

BUILT WITH TECHNOLOGY

Technology Behind the Platform

Anonymous Campus is developed using HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the frontend, with Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Firebase Cloud Functions supporting authentication, data storage and backend operations.
